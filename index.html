Gravity Hex: privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Gravity Hex ("the game") is published by HYPERTEK, ul. Mioceńska 2 m.3, 97-400 Bełchatów, Poland. Contact for privacy questions: janusz.kina@hypertek.pl.

What the game itself collects

Nothing is sent to us. The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no server of its own. It stores a small amount of data only on your device:

  • your best scores and daily results,
  • game settings (sound, music, controls),
  • whether you have finished the tutorial,
  • counters that decide when an ad may be shown (finished games and minutes played since the last ad, and session timing),
  • your ad consent choices, which are stored by Google's consent tool (see below),
  • whether you bought "Remove ads forever" (a copy of what the App Store / Google Play report),
  • simple totals that never leave your device: how many ads you have seen, how often you opened Settings or the "Remove ads" offer, how many games you finished, how many purchases you started, cancelled or completed, and the date you first opened the game,
  • when the game last showed you its "No ads" offer and how many times you passed on it, so that it is not shown too often,
  • only if you take part in a test session run by the developer: a short participant code typed into a hidden observer panel, and a local log of your games. If nobody enters a code, nothing is recorded.

This data stays on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising: Google AdMob

The free game shows full-screen ads between games, served by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited). No ad is shown during your first session or during play. To serve and measure ads, the AdMob SDK may collect and process:

  • device and advertising identifiers (on iOS, the IDFA only if you allow tracking; on Android, the advertising ID),
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it,
  • device information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • ad interaction data (which ads were shown or tapped),
  • diagnostic data about the SDK's own performance.

Google processes this data under its own policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Ads in this game are limited to content suitable for general audiences (Google's "G" ad content rating).

Your choices and consent

  • European Economic Area, UK and Switzerland: before any ad is requested, the game shows Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform). You can accept, reject or choose purposes. If you reject, you may still see non-personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → PRIVACY in the game.
  • iOS: the game asks your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before your device's advertising identifier can be used for personalised ads. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's Google settings.
  • Selling data: we receive no personal data from the game and do not sell it. Data that Google collects for advertising is governed by the Google policy linked above.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. The game is rated for ages 13 and over.

In-app purchases

Payments are handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details. The game keeps only an "ads removed" flag on your device and asks the store again at every launch, so the store remains the source of truth.

Unity's in-app purchasing SDK (Unity Technologies) receives purchase event data: installation and session identifiers, device and OS details, the app's bundle id and version, the install time, the transaction identifier and the store receipt, and on iOS the identifier for vendor. It uses them to operate and report on purchases. Unity's policy: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of personal data. Because the game sends no data to us, data held by Google for advertising is best managed through the consent options above and Google's own tools. You may also contact us at janusz.kina@hypertek.pl, and you may complain to your data protection authority (in Poland: Prezes UODO, https://uodo.gov.pl).

Changes

We will update this page when the game changes what it collects, and change the date at the top.
